# Missing information: 30-card lookup checklist

Based on the initial research catalogue dated 15 September 2026. These are gaps in our research, not statements that issuers withhold the information.

## How to use this list

For each answer, save the official URL and the date checked. Write **None** only if confirmed, **Not published** if the issuer does not publish it, and **Not applicable** when the field does not apply. For welcome offers, include amount, reward currency, spending target, deadline, expiry and customer restrictions. A personalised offer is not guaranteed for everyone.

## Checks shared by every card

- **Payment network:** Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover, or variant-dependent. This field is empty for all 30.
- **Benefits:** the structured perks list is empty for all 30. Record the principal useful benefits, their limits and any enrolment/spending requirements.
- **Full rewards conditions:** every schedule is marked incomplete. Check category exclusions, caps, shared caps, activation, portal requirements, introductory periods and base rate. Existing captured rates are shown below.
- **Application availability and eligibility:** no card has been fully verified as open to new applicants. Confirm country/residency, age, student status, income or banking relationship requirements, plus relevant restrictions. Partial eligibility notes are shown below.
- **Credit-score minimum:** empty for all 30. Only record an explicit issuer-published threshold; otherwise write Not published. Do not invent one or treat a third-party recommended score as an approval cutoff.

## Fee fields that need interpretation

If an annual fee is known, a blank monthly-fee field does not automatically mean a second fee needs researching; likewise for monthly-billed products. Confirm the actual billing structure. First-year fee terms are not captured unless explicitly shown below. Check whether the normal fee applies immediately or is waived/discounted.
